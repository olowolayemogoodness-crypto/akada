# Code of conduct

Edulink Prep is built by and for students and teachers. Many contributors are students themselves.

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Critique work, never people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexual content or personal attacks, in any language.
- Keep learning content accurate, original and appropriate for secondary-school students.

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in the README. Reports are kept confidential.
